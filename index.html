    How the signal works

    The app scores gold on five rules using daily closing prices, with today's live price as a provisional close. The total runs from −8 (strong sell) to +8 (strong buy).

    • Discount from the record: 20%+ below the all-time high scores +2, 10%+ scores +1, within 3% of it scores −1.
    • Momentum (RSI 14-day): under 30 is oversold (+2), under 40 (+1), over 60 (−1), over 70 is overbought (−2).
    • Long-term trend: price above its 200-day average scores +1, below it −1.
    • Trend direction: 50-day average above the 200-day (+1) or below it (−1).
    • Overstretch: more than 12% above the 200-day average (−1), more than 20% (−2).

    Peaks and troughs mark swings that reversed by at least 3–20% depending on the range. Signals look backwards and can't predict shocks. The coloured strip under the chart shows what the signal said each day.
